Team Takeaways

💬
Words with weight. "Nice job" doesn't cut it. Add specifics and sincerity — say what they did and why it mattered.
🔔
It doesn't have to be public. A thoughtful 1:1 message or a quick DM can mean just as much as a shout-out in a meeting.
🎯
Tie it to the mission. Appreciation hits hardest when it connects someone's work to a bigger goal they care about.
⏰
Consistency matters. A single moment of thanks is great; a pattern of recognition builds culture.
